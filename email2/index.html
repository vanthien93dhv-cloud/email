One simple blood test could reveal what’s next

Head to SITC booth 1119 to see why HLA-A*02:01 matters in advanced melanoma

In second-line advanced melanoma, limited options are a clinical reality. 1,2

For patients with advanced melanoma whose disease is progressing, or who are refractory to or intolerant of immune checkpoint inhibitors, subsequent standard options yield diminishing returns and outcomes remain poor. 1,3-5

Early HLA identification, through a simple blood test, can reveal a path to emerging TCR T-cell therapies.
