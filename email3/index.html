HLA-A*02:01 is the key to
accessing intracellular targets

Nearly 100% of the tumor proteome
can be targeted by TCR T-cell therapies by accessing intracellular proteins 3-5

~25% of the tumor proteome
can be recognized by conventional CAR T-cell approaches, which are limited to cell-surface proteins4,5

Among these intracellular targets is

PRAME

which is commonly expressed in melanoma with minimal expression in normal tissue6,7.

Test at diagnosis

Test every advanced melanoma patient for HLA-A*02:01 at diagnosis to help inform future treatment decisions. 14,15

For patients previously diagnosed, testing can still be done at progression. A high-resolution, four-digit test is required to distinguish HLA-A*02:01 from closely related alleles. 16,17,18

TCR T-cell therapies are HLA-guided

By presenting PRAME-derived peptides on the tumor cell surface, HLA-A*02:01 makes an otherwise inaccessible antigen visible to the engineered TCR.8-13
